### 🔬 Research & Repositories

#### 🧬 [Combining matching and counter-matching in nested case-control sampling](https://github.com/nguyen-thanhlam/matching)
> Simulation studies to compare matching vs. counter-matching sampling protocols in cohort-nested case-control designs.

#### 🦠 [Measles Immunity Gap & Sero-surveillance](https://github.com/nguyen-thanhlam/riskoutbreak)
> Sero-epidemiological data analysis and mathematical risk modelling for measles outbreak detection in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

#### 🩺 [Colorectal Cancer Prognostic Model](https://github.com/nguyen-thanhlam/polyp)
> Multivariable logistic regression and risk score modelling based on clinical parameters and patient risk factors.
